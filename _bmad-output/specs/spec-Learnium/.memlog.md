- (capability) CAP-1: Topic-to-Set generation lets authenticated users turn a valid topic into a structured Set of ordered Lessons; success is schema-valid 4-12 Lessons persisted within 60 seconds with quota consumed only after success.
- (capability) CAP-2: Lesson progression lets users complete ordered Lessons and Sets with persisted state; success is cross-session completion, no duplicate XP, exact-once Set completion, and visible n-of-m progress.
- (capability) CAP-3: Study Buddy tutoring lets users ask Nova for lesson-grounded help; success is persisted chat history, quota checked before model work and spent only after successful response, and out-of-quota users can continue the Lesson.
- (capability) CAP-4: Daily Goal and Streak loop lets users select an XP target, receive reminders, and preserve a streak through quick learning; success is local-time goal tracking, in-session streak update, soft loss state, and reminder deep-link to the fastest goal action.
- (capability) CAP-5: Rewards system awards XP, Levels, and Badges from server-authoritative events; success is idempotent XP, threshold-derived Levels, and one-time Badge awards with dates.
- (capability) CAP-6: Review Sessions provide spaced-repetition practice from completed Lessons; success is a 5-10 question session under about 3 minutes, zero generation/chat quota cost, XP credit, and schedule updates by answer result.
- (capability) CAP-7: Learning Paths organize broader goals into ordered Sets; success is a 3-8 Set outline with first Set generated, visible path progress, lazy next-Set generation subject to quota, and Path completion celebration.
- (capability) CAP-8: Leagues and social proof let users compare progress without becoming a social network; success is weekly cohorts, opt-out, privacy-limited standings, public profiles private by default, share cards, and friends leaderboard.
- (capability) CAP-9: Free/Plus billing and quota enforcement lets users upgrade and manage subscriptions around generation and Buddy limits; success is visible quota state, Stripe-confirmed entitlement, return to interrupted action, and no progress deletion on downgrade.
- (capability) CAP-10: Account and privacy controls let users manage age-gated accounts and delete personal data; success is 16+ signup gate and account deletion removing personal data and chat history within 30 days.
- (constraint) Generated learning content and Buddy responses must reject unsafe/gibberish/non-generatable topics before quota spend, stay educational, and expose report controls on every Lesson.
- (constraint) Anti-hallucination controls are launch-blocking: schema validation, minimum substance, input-language match, lesson grounding, report triage, and AI-generated disclosure on every Set.
- (constraint) All LLM-touching actions are cost-controlled by quota gates, semantic cache where applicable, or lazy generation; Review Sessions must not require live generation or chat quota.
- (constraint) Privacy defaults are restrictive: learning history and chats are personal data, not used to train third-party models, never exposed socially beyond explicit limited projections, and users are minimum age 16.
- (constraint) V1 is responsive web only and must be excellent on phones; no native mobile apps, enterprise admin, credentials, or UGC platform are in scope.
- (constraint) Implementation must preserve server authority for auth, quota, rewards, ownership, social projections, scheduled jobs, Stripe entitlements, generated content atomicity, and time boundaries as defined by the architecture spine.
- (constraint) Design must follow the adopted Learnium DESIGN.md tokens and EXPERIENCE.md behavioral rules; light and dark are co-equal and Nova is the North Star Study Buddy voice.
